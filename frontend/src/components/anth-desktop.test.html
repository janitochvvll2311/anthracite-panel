Draggable, resizable, minimizable windows. Each window is a view over one entry the desktop owns, so the layout survives a reload and a minimized panel is detached rather than hidden. The demos report their own connect and disconnect counts: a minimized panel stops counting.

Try it

  • Open two panels, then drag the title bars around.
  • Drag a window past an edge: it stops with its title bar still on screen.
  • Resize from the bottom-right corner; it cannot collapse.
  • Minimize a window and watch its counter freeze, then restore it from the taskbar and watch the panel resume in place.
  • Click a window behind another: it comes to the front.
  • Reload the page: the arrangement and the z order come back.
  • Press the pop-out button: the panel opens at #/window/<id> with no shell around it.

Minimized means detached

A hidden element keeps its timers, its stream and its WebSocket. The desktop removes a minimized body from the document instead, which fires disconnectedCallback in the demo panel and stops its counter. Restoring appends the same instance, so state survives.
